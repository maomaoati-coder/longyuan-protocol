---

## The Problem Nobody Wants to Say Out Loud

Artificial intelligence is not "changing" the workforce. It is **replacing** it.

Not slowly. Not partially. Entirely.

When that happens — and it is already happening — the entire economic contract that human civilization has operated under for centuries collapses:

```
You work → You earn → You spend → Economy functions
```

Remove the first step, and everything after it fails.

Governments will print money. Banks will keep lending. Economists will propose UBI.

**None of these solve the root problem.**

Printing money gives power to whoever controls the printer.  
Banks profit from deposits they never earned the right to use.  
UBI funded by tax still depends on someone having income to tax.

---

## What Is Longyuan (龙元)?

Longyuan is a post-labor monetary protocol designed for the world after AI displacement.

**Core rules:**

- 1 LONGYUAN = 1.5 USD (fixed anchor)
- Every human receives 100,000 LY at birth / system launch
- Spending on **daily life necessities** generates automatic interest returns (6–8%)
- Large purchases (real estate, vehicles, luxury) generate zero interest
- No banks. No intermediaries. No printers.
- Issuance is governed entirely by AI Labor Tax — collected from corporations whose AI systems replace human workers

**The mechanism:**

```
AI replaces 100 workers
→ 40% of those workers' former wages → Longyuan Pool
→ Pool distributes interest to all daily spenders
→ The more you live, the more you earn back
→ No job required
```
